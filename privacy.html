Werkly

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Werkly helps students find working-student positions in Germany and prepare their applications. The app can be used in guest mode. Creating an account, uploading a CV, sharing a location and using generative AI are optional.

Data we process

  • Account: email address, technical user ID and an optional name.
  • Professional profile: education, skills, languages, city, work preferences and professional summary.
  • Documents: a CV uploaded voluntarily by the user.
  • App activity: favorites, application status, reminders and the actions needed to synchronize these features.
  • Location: when permission is granted, the approximate device position is converted on the device to the nearest supported city. Werkly saves the city, not the raw device coordinates.
  • AI content: questions and the professional or vacancy context needed to answer them, only after consent.
  • AI reports: the reported answer, a reason and optional details when a signed-in user flags generated content.
  • Employer submissions: contact details and vacancy content submitted for moderation. Contact details are not displayed publicly.

Why we process it

We use this information to operate accounts, create professional profiles, find and rank vacancies, explain compatibility scores, estimate journeys, synchronize favorites and application tracking, generate requested application material, send security emails and protect the service from abuse.

Guest mode and data stored on the device

In guest mode, favorites, application status, saved searches, commute cache and notification schedules remain on the device. They are synchronized with an account only after the user signs in and chooses the corresponding features.

Storage and security

Account and application data are hosted with Supabase in the European Union. CVs are stored in a private bucket. Connections use HTTPS, and database access rules restrict signed-in users to their own private records. No advertising or analytics SDK is included in the app.

External services

  • Supabase: authentication, database, private file storage and server functions.
  • Configured email provider: account confirmation and password-recovery emails.
  • OpenStreetMap: map display. Public OSRM routing can receive city-centre coordinates to estimate a journey; it does not receive the user's raw device position.
  • Google Gemini, optional: when a user explicitly starts CV analysis, the CV file is sent for analysis and the extracted profile is saved. When a user asks the AI assistant for generated help, the question, relevant professional profile and vacancy context are sent. The normal assistant request does not include the user's name, email address or CV file. AI features remain optional and require a disclosure and consent step.
  • Vacancy providers: Werkly displays public vacancies from the Bundesagentur für Arbeit, Adzuna and public company career pages such as Greenhouse and Lever. Opening an original vacancy takes the user to its provider.

The data practices and retention rules of an external service are also governed by that service's terms and privacy policy. In particular, content sent through a free generative-AI service may be processed by its provider to improve its products; users should not enter sensitive information in AI prompts.

Legal basis and user choice

Account and core service data are processed to provide the service requested by the user. Optional location, CV analysis and generative-AI features are initiated by the user and protected by an in-context disclosure and consent choice. Consent can be refused without losing guest job search.

Retention and deletion

Account data are kept while the account exists, unless a longer period is required by law. Users can edit their profile and permanently delete the account, private CV and associated data from Profile → Account and privacy → Delete my account. Deletion can also be started from the Werkly account-deletion page. Local guest data can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Automated recommendations

Compatibility scores are informative recommendations based on profile, skills and preferences. They are not hiring decisions, do not automatically exclude vacancies and can be imperfect.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection concerning their personal data. They may also withdraw optional consent for future processing.

Deutsch · Kurzfassung

Werkly kann ohne Konto genutzt werden. Konto, CV-Upload, Standort und generative KI sind optional. Bei erteilter Standortfreigabe wird die Position lokal in eine nahe Stadt umgewandelt; gespeichert wird nur die Stadt. Eine CV-Datei wird nur nach ausdrücklicher Zustimmung zur Analyse an Google Gemini übertragen. Konto und zugehörige Daten können in der App oder über die Webseite zur Kontolöschung gelöscht werden.

Français · Résumé

Werkly fonctionne sans compte. La création d’un compte, l’envoi d’un CV, la localisation et l’IA générative sont facultatifs. Après autorisation, la position est convertie localement en une ville proche et seule cette ville est enregistrée. Le CV n’est transmis à Google Gemini qu’après un consentement explicite à son analyse. Le compte et les données associées peuvent être supprimés dans l’application ou depuis la page Web de suppression.
