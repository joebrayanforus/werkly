Delete your Werkly account and data

You can permanently delete your account without reinstalling the mobile app by using the Werkly web application.

  1. Open Werkly and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile, then Account and privacy.
  3. Select Delete my account and confirm.

Open Werkly

What is deleted?

The account, professional profile, private CV, favorites, application tracking, AI usage counters and employer submissions associated with that account are deleted. The operation cannot be undone.

Deutsch

Öffne die Werkly-Web-App, melde dich an und wähle Profil → Konto und Datenschutz → Mein Konto löschen. Dein Konto und die damit verbundenen Daten werden dauerhaft gelöscht.

Français

Ouvre l’application Web Werkly, connecte-toi puis sélectionne Profil → Compte et confidentialité → Supprimer mon compte. Le compte et les données associées seront définitivement supprimés.

Need help?

If you cannot sign in, send a deletion request from the email address associated with the account to joeoumbe@gmail.com. We may ask you to verify ownership before deleting the account.
